

### Color Spaces

red, green, blue is a nice color representation when working with monitors
• However, for other tasks it is preferable to work with a different representation of the same color, Fx.
– CMY (Cyan-Magenta-Yellow): Printing
– HLS (Hue-Luminance-Saturation): Color segmentation
– HSV, HSI: alternatives to HLS
– YUV: Analog television signals, Reduce hue quantisation
– CIE XYZ: Standard colorspace, encompases all visible colors
– CIE LUV: Color differences correspond with perception
