## Decibel 

Voltage gain in Decibel

$$A_{vDb}=20*\log\left( \frac{v_{o}}{v_{in}} \right)$$
Power gain in Decibel
$$A_{pDb}=10*\log\left( \frac{P_{0}}{P_{in}} \right)$$
